Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, withdraw your consent, or stop receiving emails, by writing to dutystitch@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. Every email we send will include a way to unsubscribe. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India or, depending on where you live, to your own data-protection authority.
