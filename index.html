Selling to US buyers from abroad now means doing customs maths on every listing

You prepay the duty

Since 9 July 2026, Etsy expects sellers outside the US to include duties and import fees in their upfront price, using a shipping option that prepays them.

Etsy's announcement

Etsy's calculator stops at variations

Etsy's US tariff calculator works on one listing at a time, and Etsy says it is not available for listings with price variations.

Etsy Help

The rates keep moving

US tariff rules changed several times during 2026. A US price you worked out a few months ago may no longer cover your costs.

Federal Register notice, 28 July 2026

What we plan to build

  • A suggested US tariff code for each item, linked to the official US schedule, for you to confirm.
  • An estimate of the duty and fees you would prepay.
  • A US price for every listing and every variation that keeps your profit where it was.
  • A note when a tariff change means some prices should be looked at again.
  • Results you can edit and download as a spreadsheet.

How it is intended to work

This is the plan. Your answers on the waitlist form will change it.

  1. Download your listings from Etsy

    Etsy lets you download a file of your listings from Shop Manager. No Etsy login is shared with us.

  2. Open the file in Dutystitch

    Each item gets a suggested tariff code and an estimated duty, with a link to check the code yourself.

  3. Review and adjust

    Confirm or change codes, add your carrier's fee, and see a US price for every listing and variation.

  4. Update your prices and stay current

    Download the results and update your US prices in Etsy. When rates change, you are told which prices to revisit.

Who this is for

A good fit

  • Your Etsy shop is based outside the United States.
  • You sell physical items and ship to US buyers.
  • You have a lot of listings, or listings with several priced variations.

Probably not for you

  • You are based in the US.
  • You only sell digital downloads.
  • You have a few single-price listings. Etsy's own calculator may be all you need.

Questions sellers might ask

Can I use Dutystitch today?

No. It is not built. This page exists to find out whether sellers want it and what it should do first.

Doesn't Etsy already have a tariff calculator?

Yes, and for single-price listings it may be enough. Etsy says it is not available for listings with price variations, and it works one listing at a time. We are aiming at whole shops and variation listings.

Will the tariff codes be official?

No. They would be suggestions for you to check against the official US tariff schedule. Dutystitch will not be a customs broker and will not give customs advice. You stay responsible for what you declare.

Will it need access to my Etsy account?

The plan is no. It would start from the listings file Etsy lets you download yourself.

What will it cost?

No price has been set. Joining the waitlist is free and does not commit you to anything.

Are you connected to Etsy?

No. Dutystitch is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Etsy.

What do you do with my answers?

We use them to decide whether and what to build, and to email you about the waitlist. The privacy notice has the detail.
