Is there still a duty-free limit for small parcels to the US?

Last checked: 16 November 2026.

No. The $800 allowance that let small parcels into the United States free of duty has been suspended for goods from every country since 29 August 2025. A low-value parcel from an Etsy shop abroad can now owe duty like any other import.

What the allowance was

US law let most shipments worth $800 or less enter without duty. It is often called the "de minimis" exemption, and it sits in the tariff law at 19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(C). For years it meant that a single handmade item posted to a US buyer arrived with nothing to pay.

What changed and when

Executive Order 14324, "Suspending Duty-Free De Minimis Treatment for All Countries", was signed on 30 July 2025 and published in the Federal Register on 5 August 2025. It says the exemption "shall no longer apply", "regardless of value, country of origin, mode of transportation, or method of entry", for goods entered "on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern daylight time on August 29, 2025."

The order set out separate, temporary arrangements for parcels sent through the postal network.

Since then the Federal Register has published further documents continuing the suspension: an order in February 2026 titled "Continuing the Suspension of Duty-Free De Minimis Treatment for All Countries", and two rules from US Customs and Border Protection on 24 June 2026 titled "Indefinite Suspension of the De Minimis Exemption", one for mail and one for all other ways of shipping. We have read the 2025 order in full; for the 2026 documents we are relying on their titles and summaries, so read them yourself if the detail matters to you.

What it means for a small parcel

  • Value no longer decides whether duty is owed. A £20 item and a £200 item are both subject to it.
  • How much is owed depends on what the item is and where it was made, not on a flat threshold.
  • Post is not a way round it. The arrangements for mail have changed more than once; ask your postal service or carrier how duty is collected on your parcels today.
  • On Etsy, the duty is yours to prepay. What Etsy expects.

What to do

  1. Stop assuming low-value orders are duty-free. Price every US order with duty in mind. The costs to include.
  2. Check that your shipping service to the US prepays duty. A pre-shipping checklist.
  3. If you paused US sales when this began, the sums for raising prices or absorbing the cost show what restarting would take.

Where to check

Search the Federal Register for "de minimis" to see the orders and rules in date order, and ask your carrier how it applies them.
