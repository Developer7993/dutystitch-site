What happens if my US buyer is charged duty at the door?

Last checked: 16 November 2026.

The buyer can be refunded, and the charge is deducted from you. Etsy tells US buyers they should not be asked to pay anything more when an order arrives, because sellers are required to prepay duty. If a carrier charges the buyer anyway, Etsy's rules put the cost back on the seller.

What Etsy tells buyers

Etsy's help page for buyers, "Will I Have to Pay for Tax, Customs, or Tariffs on My Order?", says: "Sellers are required to use Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) shipping for orders sent to the US." It tells US buyers "you shouldn't be asked to pay any additional charges when your order arrives", and that a buyer who is charged should contact the seller and, if that does not resolve it, open a case with Etsy.

What Etsy tells sellers

Etsy's announcement to sellers is direct about the consequences:

  • "Orders shipped without duties prepaid will no longer qualify for Etsy Purchase Protection."
  • If buyers are charged tariffs or collection fees by the carrier, "they're eligible to be refunded and the charges will be deducted from the seller."

What it costs you

An example with made-up numbers. You sell an item for £30 and ship it without prepaying duty. The carrier asks the buyer for £3 duty and adds a £9 collection fee. The buyer is refunded £12 and £12 is deducted from you.

Had you prepaid, the cost would have been the £3 duty plus your carrier's fee for prepaying, which is usually less than a collection fee charged at the door. You would also have kept Purchase Protection on the order, and the buyer would not have had a surprise.

Collection fees vary by carrier. Check your own carrier's price list; the figure above is only an illustration.

How to avoid it

  1. Use a shipping service that prepays duty to the US. More on the rule.
  2. Put the duty, the carrier's fee and the extra marketplace fees into your US price, so prepaying does not come out of your income. The costs to include.
  3. Before a parcel leaves, check the label is the duties-prepaid kind. A pre-shipping checklist.

If it has already happened

  • Reply to the buyer quickly and plainly. They were told they would not be charged.
  • Check which shipping service the parcel went on, so you know whether it was the wrong label or a carrier error.
  • If you bought a duties-prepaid service and the buyer was still charged, raise it with the carrier and keep the evidence.
  • Fix the shipping profile before the next US order.
